I took the **Rlaarlo Omni Terminator** straight to the skatepark to explore jumping and what my conventional metal-chassis car could withstand. I finished happy, although the session brought two problems and braking that needed attention.

<!--more-->

## Version and Package Received

The video distinguishes this version from the Carbon. Mine included a **2200mAh 3S XT60 battery**, transmitter, USB charger, spare parts and seals, tools and a wheelie bar to fit. Wheels were separate and I had to finish applying body stickers.

That was my package, rather than guaranteed contents for every current offer. Check the selected version and package.

It is marketed as mini 1/10, but looked surprisingly close in size to my 1/12 buggy. Actual dimensions and transport needs matter more to me than advertised scale alone.

## Ready for Fun

I like the metal chassis, hex screws, receiver box and finish details, plus lighting and anti-roll bars.

Suspension mounting positions and preload are adjustable, but the arms lack the range of adjustable links. My purpose was jumping and fun, rather than competition setup.

I fitted the wheelie bar and used the supplied electronics. **This test precedes the later replacement-ESC preparation**. Do not mix the two configurations when assessing stock behaviour.

## What Happened During Jumps

The session included harsh landings and attempts at good footage. The overall impression was very positive: the car kept running without a structural break ending the day.

It did not emerge intact:

- **The ESC came unstuck**, requiring a stop to secure it.
- **A front-light wire broke**, stopping the lights.

Those were specific failures on my outing. A good overall jumping result does not justify calling it indestructible.

## Braking Was My Least Favourite Part

Braking felt abrupt, especially when correcting attitude in the air. Sometimes releasing or slightly moving the trigger seemed to apply excessive braking.

I had not isolated the cause in this review and planned transmitter/electronics checks. This is not a settled diagnosis for all Omni Terminators.

There is no speed or runtime measurement here. The conclusions concern skatepark use.

## What Happened Next

I kept the car because I liked its size and jumping fun, allowing for securing components and investigating braking.

Later I reinforced the body and changed the ESC.
