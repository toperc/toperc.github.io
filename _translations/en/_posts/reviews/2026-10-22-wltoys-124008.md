Good reviews gave me high expectations for the **Wltoys 124008**. My experience differed: throttle response and skatepark breakages left me unwilling to keep the complete car.

<!--more-->

## What I Saw on Opening

It is a brushless four-wheel-drive buggy with plastic chassis and front/rear differentials. Its enclosed-format battery was inconvenient with my usual chargers.

Suspension bounced considerably. Fixed links offered less adjustment than I wanted. The transmission interested me because I had used its components in [124017/144010 upgrades]({{ '/manuales/mejoras-wltoys-124017-144010/' | relative_url }}).

That does not establish compatibility for every similar-looking spare; those adaptations are documented separately.

## Electronics Response Did Not Convince Me

From initial checks I noticed less response than expected and an apparent throttle delay, while steering seemed quick.

This is a user assessment: **I did not instrumentally measure latency or top speed**, nor diagnose a specific ESC fault from impressions.

## Asphalt and Dirt

I could control it on asphalt. Dirt produced more nervous reactions, making me consider differential work. That adjustment was neither completed nor compared in this test, so remains a possibility rather than demonstrated solution.

My personal reference was the [Wltoys 124017]({{ '/reviews/wltoys-124017/' | relative_url }}), which I enjoyed more, rather than two identically configured laboratory test cars.

## What Happened at the Skatepark

After the first fall discussed, I already found wing and suspension damage. Later inspection also showed heavy spur wear and a bent motor shaft. Subsequent launches were harsher after the car was already damaged.

Airborne throttle corrections were difficult too. I could not raise the nose as expected; braking tipped it forward.

**The footage cannot establish which part started every problem or exclude a bad landing's influence.** I acknowledge that possibility. This was one car and session, rather than a controlled durability test of the entire range.

## My Conclusion After Dismantling

I would not recommend it based on my experience. I chose to reuse parts in other projects rather than rebuild it completely.

The purchase link identifies the video's model; it does not change that conclusion or imply inspection of packages sold today.
