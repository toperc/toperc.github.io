The **Wltoys 144010** is an updated version of the popular 144001, taking the RC driving experience to a new level. With improvements to the motor and control, it is designed for those seeking speed and precision without sacrificing durability. After testing it on different terrain, here is what makes this model stand out.

<!--more-->

## Main Specifications

- **Scale:** 1/14
- **Top speed:** Up to 75 km/h
- **Motor:** High-power brushless motor
- **Suspension:** Fully adjustable oil-filled shocks
- **Battery:** 7.4V 2600mAh LiPo
- **Chassis material:** Reinforced aluminium for greater stability

## Design and Build

The Wltoys 144010 retains the rugged structure of the 144001 but adds several performance improvements. The reinforced aluminium chassis looks professional and improves stability at high speed. An aerodynamic body and bright colours give it a modern, aggressive appearance.

Independent suspension and adjustable oil-filled shocks provide excellent handling on rough ground. The work put into creating a smoother driving experience is noticeable even in difficult conditions.

## Driving Performance

Performance is the Wltoys 144010's strong point. Its brushless motor can take it up to 75 km/h, making it one of the fastest cars in its class. Acceleration is immediate and steering response is precise, allowing controlled turns and impressive stability, particularly on smooth surfaces.

On dirt or gravel, the Wltoys 144010 maintains excellent traction. The tyres and suspension work together to soften bumps and keep you in control. Despite its speed, it remains stable and delivers smooth, fluid driving.

## Battery Life

The 7.4V 2600mAh LiPo battery provides a notable 20–25 minutes of use at full power. This improvement is a major advantage if you want longer sessions without constantly recharging.

## Maintenance and Spare Parts

Spare parts and upgrades are another advantage. As an improved version of the 144001, many parts are compatible and easy to find. Its modular design also makes it straightforward to dismantle for maintenance and adjustments.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros:**

- Brushless motor for greater speed and durability.
- Longer battery life than earlier models.
- Excellent traction and stability on different terrain.
- Rugged, aerodynamic design.

**Cons:**

- Its speed can challenge beginners.
- More expensive than the 144001, although the improvements help justify it.

## Conclusion

The Wltoys 144010 is an excellent option if you want speed, control and durability. Its brushless motor and rugged design suit intermediate and experienced drivers wanting to tackle difficult terrain without losing performance. It costs a little more than the 144001, but the improvements justify the investment. It is a versatile, powerful RC car that meets expectations.
