HobbyKing sent this **Rapid LiHV 2S** through the channel partnership. I took it to the circuit to compare its use with my LRP and see how long I could drive.

<!--more-->

## An Important Difference Between Video and Link

In the recording I identify the tested pack as **6400mAh**. The description URL identifies a **6000mAh** Rapid. I have no confirmation that they are exactly the same product.

I retain the video's original link below without automatically transferring every test result to that variant. Check the store's actual product and specifications before buying.

## Reception and Connection

Initial checks showed both cells near 3.8V. Its power and balance connections require correct polarity.

The video notes missing information in the instructions and includes charging assumptions. **Do not infer maximum voltage from the letters LiHV alone:** confirm the exact reference and charger/electronics compatibility. This entry offers no charging setting for an unidentified pack.

## Sessions Were Not Identical

The track started wet with little grip. The Rapid ran about 30 minutes; afterwards the 5000mAh LRP ran 21 minutes 47 seconds. The track dried between sessions and my pace changed.

These describe events, but **do not alone prove an exact capacity or efficiency difference**. Neither surface nor driving remained constant.

## What the Lap Times Say

My final Rapid session on a drier track improved times. I mention about 25.9 seconds average and a best lap of 24.98 seconds.

I was pleased, but cannot attribute the improvement solely to the battery because grip changed too. The charger's replenished mAh are not a standardised capacity test either.

## How I Finished Feeling

I was happy with the experience, running time and received pack's operation. I certify neither discharge C nor internal-resistance curves.
