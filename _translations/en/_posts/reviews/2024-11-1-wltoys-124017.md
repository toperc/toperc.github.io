The **Wltoys 124017** is a 1:12 scale RC car that takes driving to another level with its brushless motor. Designed for high-speed performance and power on different terrain, it is an excellent option for beginners and experienced drivers alike. Here are my impressions after testing it on several surfaces.

<!--more-->

## Main Specifications

- **Scale:** 1/12
- **Top speed:** Up to 75 km/h
- **Motor:** High-power brushless motor
- **Suspension:** Four-wheel independent suspension with adjustable oil-filled shocks
- **Battery:** 7.4V 2200mAh LiPo
- **Charging time:** 1.5–2 hours for a full charge
- **Run time:** Approximately 15–20 minutes
- **Chassis:** Reinforced aluminium, ideal for uneven terrain

## Design and Build

The Wltoys 124017 is designed for strength and durability. Its reinforced aluminium chassis handles impacts and tough conditions, while the brushless motor provides the extra power to reach impressive speeds of up to 75 km/h. The structure is compact but rugged, and the body details give it a professional, aggressive look.

Adjustable oil-filled shocks and four-wheel independent suspension keep the car stable on uneven terrain and absorb impacts well. This is particularly useful on dirt, gravel and small ramps.

## Driving Performance

The Wltoys 124017 performs very well on almost any surface. Speed is one of its strengths: at full throttle you can feel the brushless motor responding immediately. On flat asphalt it reaches its top speed easily, while its suspension maintains control and stability on rougher ground.

It also stands out on dirt and gravel, where the wide tyres provide good traction and handling. Care is needed at high speed on very rough ground, as jumps and bumps can momentarily unsettle it. Even so, the Wltoys 124017 adapts well to most surfaces and delivers an exciting, smooth experience.

## Battery Life

The 7.4V 2200mAh battery offers around 15–20 minutes of use at full power, a good result for this type of RC car. Charging takes around 1.5–2 hours. For longer sessions, I would recommend investing in an extra battery to maximise driving time.

## Maintenance and Spare Parts

The Wltoys 124017 is fairly easy to maintain, and many spare parts and upgrades are available. As a popular model, replacement tyres, motors and shocks are easy to find. This is useful both for repairs and for anyone wanting to personalise or improve their car.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros:**

- Brushless motor for exceptional power and speed.
- Durable aluminium chassis that resists impacts.
- Adjustable suspension for different terrain.
- Spare parts and upgrades are easy to find.

**Cons:**

- Limited battery life at full speed.
- Can lose stability at high speed on very rough terrain.

## Conclusion

The Wltoys 124017 is an excellent RC car for anyone seeking speed, control and durability in one package. Its brushless motor and rugged chassis suit beginners wanting a powerful car and experienced users seeking high performance. Battery life could be better, but its overall performance and adaptability make each run worthwhile. It offers a versatile and exciting RC experience.
