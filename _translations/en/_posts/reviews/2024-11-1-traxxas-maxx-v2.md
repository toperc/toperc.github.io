The **Traxxas Maxx V2** is an off-road RC car that takes power and durability to another level. Designed for challenging terrain and impressive speeds, it suits enthusiasts looking for excitement on every run. After several days testing it on different surfaces, here is what makes the Traxxas Maxx V2 stand out.

<!--more-->

## Main Specifications

- **Scale:** 1/10
- **Top speed:** Up to 100 km/h with a battery upgrade
- **Motor:** Velineon 540XL brushless
- **Suspension:** Long-travel shocks with a double-arm system
- **Battery:** Compatible with 4S LiPo; a 5000mAh 4S battery is recommended
- **Chassis:** Tough, impact-resistant plastic

## Design and Build

The Traxxas Maxx V2 has a large, rugged design for tackling difficult terrain. Its tough plastic chassis withstands heavy impacts and jumps. The long-travel suspension provides smooth, stable driving even on rough ground.

Another highlight is its clipless body mounting system, which makes access easy and avoids lost body clips. Grippy off-road tyres improve traction and control, keeping the Maxx V2 stable on both smooth surfaces and rough tracks.

## Driving Performance

Speed and power are where the Traxxas Maxx V2 really shines. With its Velineon 540XL brushless motor and support for 4S LiPo batteries, it can reach up to 100 km/h, particularly on flat surfaces. Acceleration is quick and the controls are precise, allowing confident high-speed manoeuvres.

On gravel, dirt and grass, the Maxx V2 performs exceptionally well. Its suspension absorbs impacts, and the structure allows jumps and manoeuvres without losing control. It is designed to overcome obstacles and keep moving, making it an appealing choice for off-road fans.

## Battery Life

With a 5000mAh 4S battery, the Traxxas Maxx V2 offers solid performance and around 15–20 minutes of use at full power. This may seem short, but reflects its power and speed. You can extend your sessions with a higher-capacity battery or additional packs.

## Maintenance and Spare Parts

The Maxx V2 is easy to maintain thanks to its modular design and accessible components. Traxxas is also known for its wide availability of spare parts and upgrades, letting you personalise the model. From the motor to the suspension, each part can be replaced, supporting the car's long-term durability.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros:**

- Extremely powerful brushless motor.
- Supports 4S batteries for higher speed.
- Tough suspension that works well on difficult terrain.
- Clipless design for quick access inside.

**Cons:**

- The battery can drain quickly at full speed.
- Its size and power may be too much for beginners.

## Conclusion

The Traxxas Maxx V2 meets the expectations of demanding drivers. Its speed, power and durability make it ideal for difficult terrain and high-speed runs. Although expensive, its performance helps justify the price. If you want an RC car that can tackle challenging conditions and deliver an outstanding off-road experience, the Traxxas Maxx V2 is hard to beat.
