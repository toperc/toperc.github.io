The **5000mAh Turnigy Rapid 2S** was one of the batteries I bought to check HobbyKing ordering myself. Although this review sits within the channel partnership, the recording explains that I paid for these packs.

<!--more-->

## What I Wanted for the Promoto

I wanted to retain my balance chargers and have more than one pack. With the kit battery and USB charger, I needed another charge just as I started getting comfortable with the bike.

This reference has EC5 and a separate balance lead. It fits my bike without changing its power connector. Closing the compartment requires positioning the balance lead carefully to avoid trapping it.

## A Timed Session

I recorded **12 minutes and 28 seconds**. That is this session with my riding and stabilisation enabled, not guaranteed runtime for everyone.

Afterwards I checked temperature and voltages. The stated reading was around 28–29°C. A single post-run measurement does not reveal every peak during discharge.

## Compared with My Previous Battery

I noticed no major difference in delivery or runtime against the Spektrum pack previously used. This is experience, rather than identical test cycles supporting a percentage improvement.

For me, the change is convenient charging with compatible equipment I already own and having a second pack. Check size, voltage and connection for the exact kit, even when the bike name matches.

## Charging Information Matters

The video mentions earlier batteries received at excessively low voltage. That is not an instruction to recover them with another chemistry's charging program: use the appropriate LiPo charger/mode and follow instructions.
