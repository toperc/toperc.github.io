Transporting cars, tools and cameras in several bags became tiring. I bought this **Fastrax for 1/8 RC** to collect the equipment and filmed its assembly. What looked simple became a lengthy exercise in finding where each part belonged.

<!--more-->

## No Instructions in My Box

I received the folded bag, reinforcing panels, dividers and drawer pieces. Before fitting anything, I had to identify which part belonged where.

The video retains my uncertainty and several wrong attempts. It is not perfect first-time assembly, but may help identify parts and avoid some of my trial and error.

## Drawers and Internal Structure

I folded the drawer panels and fitted their shaping pieces, then organised runners and dividers so the drawers were supported and slid inside the bag.

Trial-fitting before closing everything helped most. Some similar-looking parts have different lengths and positions.

## The Zip Seemed Too Tight

Initially I worried about closing tension. Inspection found one panel insufficiently seated at the bottom; repositioning it allowed closure.

The final section, recorded after substantial use, explains that **the zips no longer gave me that worrying impression**. My initial fear should not be presented as a later breakage.

## Organising Equipment

I show tools in upper and side areas, charger and cables, spares, silicone fluids and recording equipment. The large compartment holds two 1/10 cars together in my example.

Marketing for 1/8 does not guarantee every car of that scale fits with body and wing. Check the actual vehicle volume and required interior arrangement.

Battery storage shown in the recording does not turn a drawer into a dedicated protective battery container. Follow the battery manufacturer's transport and storage conditions.

## My View After Assembly

I am happy because it makes arriving at the track organised easier. Fully loaded it is still heavy; consider that weight as well as volume.
