I wanted a 1/8 electric buggy and chose the **Team Corally Python 6S XP** in a starter package. I wanted to drive without choosing every electronic component separately.

<!--more-->

## My Purchase Contents

The package included transmitter, 5400mAh 4S battery, 2S–4S charger, adapter and tools. I also show supplied spare accessories and tool case.

That was my specific offer. **I do not claim every Python RTR includes battery and charger**, or that the recorded price is current.

## What I Observed

It has an aluminium chassis, centre differential, anti-roll bars and suspension/link adjustments. I liked the assembly's appearance and checked suspension movement before driving.

The video discusses electronics and figures, but I do not use automatic transcription to fill in inaudible numbers. The relevant configuration is the supplied 4S pack.

## The Entire Outing Is on 4S

Despite the model name, **this video contains no 6S driving test**. That possibility remained for another outing.

On 4S I was very happy with acceleration and straight-line control, and surprised to enjoy the session without missing a gyro.

This is neither a comparative buggy measurement nor competition test. Impressions belong to my car and that day's preparation.

## A Good Starting Point for Me

The package avoided several separate purchases. There are still adjustments and maintenance ahead, and racers must check equipment and rules.
