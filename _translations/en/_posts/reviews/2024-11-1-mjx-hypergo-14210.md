## Main Specifications

- **Scale:** 1/14
- **Top speed:** Up to 60 km/h on 2S and 75 km/h on 3S
- **Motor:** High-power brushless motor
- **Suspension:** Independent oil-filled shocks
- **Battery:** 7.4V 1050mAh LiPo; compatible with 2S and 3S
- **Construction:** Durable plastic frame with metal drivetrain components

## Design and Build

The MJX Hyper Go 14210 has a modern, attractive design with stylish details and a rugged finish. Its durable plastic frame resists impacts, while the metal drivetrain adds durability on difficult terrain. The independent oil-filled shocks provide a smooth, stable ride, particularly on uneven surfaces.

An adjustable suspension system lets you change the ride height for different terrain. The car also includes high-traction tyres suitable for smooth surfaces and dirt paths.

## Driving Performance

The Hyper Go 14210 does not disappoint. Its brushless motor takes it up to 60 km/h on flat surfaces, an impressive speed for its size. Acceleration is quick and control is precise, making manoeuvres easy. On gravel and dirt, the oil-filled suspension and multi-surface tyres help it maintain traction and stability.

This model works for racing on flat tracks and exploring more challenging ground, adapting well and delivering a smooth driving experience on different surfaces.

## Battery Life

The MJX Hyper Go 14210 includes a 7.4V 1050mAh 2S LiPo battery, providing 10–15 minutes of use at full speed. An extra battery is recommended for longer sessions. Charging is quick and straightforward, reducing downtime between runs.

## Maintenance and Spare Parts

The Hyper Go 14210 is easy to maintain, with spare parts available from specialist shops. Its design provides easy access to internal components, simplifying repairs and upgrades. MJX also offers accessories and optional parts to personalise the car.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros:**

- High-performance brushless motor for speed and durability.
- Adjustable suspension for different terrain.
- Rugged frame with metal drivetrain components.
- Compact, manageable design for beginners and experienced drivers.

**Cons:**

- The battery can drain quickly during intense sessions.
- Its compact size limits stability on extremely rough terrain.

## Conclusion

The MJX Hyper Go 14210 combines speed, durability and attractive design in a compact RC car. Its brushless motor and adjustable suspension suit beginners and experienced enthusiasts looking for an agile, rugged car to enjoy on varied terrain. If you want a quality RC car with both performance and style, the Hyper Go 14210 is a strong option.
