The **Wltoys K969** is a 1:28 scale RC car designed specifically for drifting. Its compact design and attention to construction details make it a strong option for beginners and drift fans. Here is my experience with this RC car and why it is a good choice for enthusiasts of the discipline.

<!--more-->

## Main Specifications

- **Scale:** 1/28
- **Top speed:** 30 km/h
- **Motor:** High-torque 130 brushed motor
- **Suspension:** Four-wheel independent suspension with adjustable damping
- **Battery:** 7.4V 400mAh Li-ion
- **Charging time:** 1–1.5 hours for a full charge
- **Run time:** Approximately 10–15 minutes

## Design and Build

The Wltoys K969 has a compact, streamlined design aimed at indoor drifting and smooth surfaces. Its tough plastic chassis keeps it light and easy to manoeuvre, while withstanding the bumps common when practising drift.

The hard tyres are designed to slide on smooth surfaces, making the K969 ideal for learning and refining drifting technique. Four-wheel independent suspension also helps control through corners, maintaining stability even in tight turns.

## Drift Performance

The Wltoys K969 delivers impressive drifting performance for its scale. The motor and hard tyres allow precise cornering, slides and 180-degree turns. Its light weight means it responds quickly to control inputs and can be steered accurately through drift manoeuvres in small spaces.

This model is specifically designed for smooth surfaces such as parquet, asphalt and polished concrete. Elsewhere, the tyres are less effective and control becomes difficult. In the right setting, however, the K969 provides a fun, satisfying drift experience.

## Battery Life

The 7.4V 400mAh battery provides 10–15 minutes of driving, enough for short drift sessions. For longer practice, I suggest an extra battery. Charging takes around 1–1.5 hours, reasonable for an RC car of this scale.

## Maintenance and Spare Parts

The Wltoys K969 has a simple construction that makes maintenance and repairs straightforward. Spare parts are available, along with accessories to personalise or improve its performance. These are useful for replacing tyres or adjusting the suspension to your drifting preferences.

## Pros and Cons

**Pros:**

- Excellent control for drifting manoeuvres.
- Compact size, ideal indoors and on smooth surfaces.
- Easy to manoeuvre, even for beginners.
- Good value for money.

**Cons:**

- Somewhat limited battery life.
- Suitable only for smooth surfaces, not uneven terrain.

## Conclusion

The Wltoys K969 is a great choice for any 1:28 scale drift enthusiast. Precise control and drift-focused design make it ideal for practising indoors or on smooth surfaces while refining your technique. Battery life and terrain suitability have limitations, but performance in the right setting makes it an appealing purchase. It is compact, capable and a good introduction to drifting.
