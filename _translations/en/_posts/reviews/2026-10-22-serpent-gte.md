While selling some cars, I found this used **electric Serpent GTE 1/8**. It starts from an RTR base but has significant modifications. From the first days, it became one of the cars I most wanted to take to the track.

<!--more-->

## Mine Does Not Represent a Stock RTR

It has a Hobbywing XR8 Pro ESC, 2400KV Hobbywing motor and sensored system, plus another servo and different body. I use two 2S batteries, one on each side.

Crucially, mine has a locked front assembly rather than the conventional RTR front differential. **Its driving feel should not automatically be attributed to the boxed model**.

## What Hooked Me

It gives me confidence to start gently and push harder as I find a rhythm. I enjoy improving my own laps and sharing the track.

Impacts still happen. I discuss usage damage and several replaced CVDs. I suspect the locked front contributes under high grip, but have not isolated that cause experimentally.

## Limiting Throttle with the MT12

My RadioMaster has no-throttle, limited and full-travel modes. The video states 44% for the intermediate mode.

That percentage is **my throttle-control setting**, not a measurement of exactly 44% electrical or mechanical motor power. I use it to match the group and feel comfortable.

See my [MT12 experience]({{ '/manuales/radiomaster-mt12-experiencia/' | relative_url }}) for the transmitter.

## Wheels and Maintenance Are Part of Track Use

When pushing hard, tyres become a frequent expense. Life depends on compound, track and driving; my stated pack count is not guaranteed lifespan.

Rear-differential access is straightforward on my car and I use grease there. The locked front changes adjustment possibilities and servicing.

## A Different Focus for My Collection

My [UDR experience]({{ '/reviews/traxxas-udr-experiencia/' | relative_url }}) explains making room. The Serpent offers what I increasingly seek: circuit driving and a rhythm I want to improve.

The video includes driving and the control-display system I have been developing. This documents my car, without certifying compliance with any specific competition rules.
