I was eager to try the **MJX 10304** after enjoying its bigger 7303 sibling. Opening it, I liked its looks and assembly details. The first track outing differed: keeping straight was difficult and a small correction could turn the car sideways.

<!--more-->

This post covers that initial test. Later work changed the outcome; see the [MJX 10304 setup guide]({{ '/manuales/ajustes-mjx-10304/' | relative_url }}).

## What I Liked on Opening

Mine had a Polo-style body, another wheel set, springs and body accessories. Underneath were separate electronics, a gyro and XT60 connector.

I particularly liked body lighting connected through contacts, avoiding a wire to unplug whenever removing it. Magnetic mounting proved less convenient than expected because careful support alignment was necessary when replacing it.

The car offers camber/alignment adjustment, shock positions and anti-roll bars. I also noticed play that raised steering concerns, without yet identifying the later handling problem's cause.

## Trouble Appeared When Correcting Direction

On asphalt it could start straight, then snap sideways when I corrected. That undermined confidence; when it responded, the correction felt late.

Those are impressions, **not a servo or transmitter latency measurement**. I suspected several causes without a settled diagnosis.

The gyro did not solve it. After trying settings I stopped wanting to push a car I was not enjoying. Recommending it as successful out of the box would misrepresent that outing.

## Lowering It Was Not Enough

I reduced travel with limit screws and checked alignment. It remained nervous and could roll over on higher-grip tyres.

My 1/10 track wheels also presented offset and clearance problems. Similar overall scale does not establish wheel suitability.

I finished by proposing differential inspection. **That work had not happened in this first video**, so I cannot attribute a solution to it yet.

## Scope of the Initial Conclusion

My poor experience concerned asphalt and the received setup. There was no complete dirt test or comparative lap timing, and I do not conclude that every car has identical adjustment.

As received, mine was not something I would recommend for uninspected out-of-box circuit use. Electronics and looks did not outweigh the driving difficulty.

## A Second Chance

I subsequently checked differentials, alignment, springs and radio, then returned to similar use. The improvement allowed straight driving, corrections and enjoyment.

Read [what I changed and the result]({{ '/manuales/ajustes-mjx-10304/' | relative_url }}) before relying on this first impression alone. The [model page]({{ '/coches/mjx-10304/' | relative_url }}) collects both stages.
