After the [first Python test on 4S]({{ '/reviews/team-corally-python-6s/' | relative_url }}), I prepared a motor fan. I wanted extra cooling before more hot-weather use and documented the complete installation.

<!--more-->

## Trial-Fit Before Tightening

The set includes a fan, adjustable mount and screws. I first positioned it around the motor to check space and cable routing.

Check the supported motor diameter and height with the body fitted. A listing saying "large motors" does not establish fit for every chassis, motor and body combination.

## Check the Power Supply

I found a spare power port on my receiver and tested operation. Before repeating this, check **polarity, BEC voltage and permissible current**, as well as the fan's voltage rating.

I did not connect the fan directly to traction-battery wires or treat the port name as a compatibility guarantee. This test concerns my receiver and fan.

## Route the Cable While the Box Is Accessible

I dismantled the necessary parts to lift the electronics box and route the cable through its entry, before refitting the support, servo and cover.

I checked for trapped wiring and transmission clearance, and reviewed antenna position. Closing the box does not by itself prove any particular water protection is retained.

## Check with the Body Fitted

With everything secured, I checked fan rotation, steering movement and car operation, then fitted the body to confirm clearance and absence of vibration during that check.

## What This Video Demonstrates

The installation works on the bench. **There is no before/after temperature table under equal load**, so I claim neither a specific temperature reduction nor measured motor-life improvement.

This is not the announced 6S driving test either; that outing remained pending. A fan does not replace monitoring temperature and reviewing gearing, assembly and conditions if the motor gets too hot.
