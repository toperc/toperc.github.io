# Privacy Notice

At *TopeRC.com* (the "website"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Notice explains our practices for collecting, using and protecting your personal information when you visit the website.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in different ways to improve your experience. This may include:

- **Personal information** you provide voluntarily, such as your name, email address and other details you choose to share when registering or commenting on the website.
- **Browsing information**, such as your IP address, browser type, visit duration and the pages you view, collected automatically to improve our services.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and improve our services and your experience.
- Send relevant information or content if you subscribe to our updates.
- Respond to your comments or questions.
- Carry out analysis and improve website functionality.

## 3. Third-Party Tools

We use the following third-party tools and services to help manage the website and improve its performance:

- **Google Tag Manager:** We use Google Tag Manager to manage website scripts and tags efficiently.
- **Google Analytics:** We use Google Analytics for usage metrics, such as visit counts, popular pages and visit duration. These data help improve our content and functionality. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information anonymously and report on website interactions.
- **Consentmanager:** This tool helps manage user privacy consent and support compliance with data protection rules. It allows you to accept or reject cookies according to your preferences.
- **Disqus:** We use Disqus to manage blog comments. Disqus may collect information about commenters and stores comments on its platform. Please review its privacy policy for more information about how it handles your data.
- **Formspree.io:** We use Formspree.io to process contact requests. When you submit a contact form, it processes and transmits the information provided. Data submitted through Formspree.io are treated confidentially and used only to respond to your enquiry.

## 4. Cookies

Our website uses **cookies** to personalise and improve your online experience. Cookies are text files stored on your device to identify your browser and remember certain information. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, although this may affect some website features.

## 5. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We do share information with external service providers, such as those listed above, that help operate the website and improve our services, provided they commit to protecting your data.

## 6. Links to External Websites

Our website may link to sites outside our control. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and recommend reviewing their privacy policies when visiting them.

## 7. Protecting Information

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, use or disclosure. However, no security system is infallible, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Notice

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Notice at any time. Please review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
