TopeRC Studio is TopeRC's local tool for organizing video projects, Shorts and publication tracking.

The TikTok integration is being configured and tested. Through Login Kit, the owner connects their account and authorizes the requested permissions. Before each transfer, they select their own video, review the destination and confirm an immediate or scheduled delivery.

Studio transfers the video to the TikTok inbox. The creator must open the notification in TikTok, review the caption, cover, visibility and commercial disclosures, and complete publication there. **Sending a video does not publish it.**

The application runs on the owner's computer. This page describes its operation; it does not provide access to the local Studio or public registration.
